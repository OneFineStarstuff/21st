# AI Strategy: Precision Agriculture & Autonomous Agronomy (2026–2030)

## Strategic Objective
Leverage the Unified AGI System to drive efficiency and sustainability in global agriculture through hybrid edge-cloud architectures and high-assurance decision-making.

## Core Pillars

### 1. Hybrid Edge-Cloud Architecture
- **Edge Perception**: Deploy EfficientNet-B0 vision models on field sensors for real-time pest and nutrient analysis.
- **Cloud Decisioning**: Utilize the Performer-based decision module in central hubs for aggregate resource planning (irrigation, fertilizer).

### 2. Autonomous Agronomy
- **MoE for Diverse Crops**: Train specialized experts in the Mixture of Experts router for different soil types and climate zones.
- **DNC Memory for Seasonal Trends**: Utilize the Memory Module to track long-term crop cycles and yield correlations.

### 3. Regulatory & Ethical Compliance
- **EU AI Act Alignment**: Ensure "High-Risk AI" compliance through the ASA Interpretability Layer (CAE).
- **Fairness in Allocation**: Apply ZK-Fairness proofs to ensure equitable resource distribution among smallholder farmers (MAS FEAT compliant).

## Implementation Roadmap
- **2026**: Pilot deployments in EU and Southeast Asia.
- **2027**: Full integration of sensor fusion (multimodal inputs) for predictive yield modeling.
- **2028**: Global rollout of autonomous agronomy agents.
